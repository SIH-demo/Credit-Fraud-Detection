Risk Configuration

Configure fraud-risk classification thresholds and review settings.

Administrator Configuration Risk thresholds determine how prediction scores are classified into Low, Medium and High categories. Changes should be evaluated using validation data before deployment.

Low Risk

Transactions with lower model-estimated fraud risk.

Score range: 0.00 to 1.00

Medium Risk

Transactions requiring additional attention or review.

Score range: 0.00 to 1.00

High Risk

Transactions with higher model-estimated fraud risk.

Score range: 0.00 to 1.00
